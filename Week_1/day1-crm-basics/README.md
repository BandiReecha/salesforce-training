# Account

An Account in Salesforce represents a company, organization, or customer business.

It is used to store details about companies with which a business interacts.

## Information Stored in Account

* Company Name
* Phone Number
* Address
* Industry
* Website

## Example

* Amazon
* Infosys
* TCS

## Real-Time Example

If a software company wants to provide services to Amazon, then Amazon is stored as an Account in Salesforce.

## Important Point

One Account can have multiple Contacts.

# Contact

A Contact in Salesforce represents a person associated with an Account.

It stores information about individual customers or employees.

## Information Stored in Contact

* Name
* Email
* Phone Number
* Designation

## Example

Employee or manager working in a company.

## Real-Time Example

If “Rahul Sharma” works at Infosys:

* Infosys = Account
* Rahul Sharma = Contact

## Important Point

Contacts are linked to Accounts.


# Opportunity

An Opportunity in Salesforce represents a potential sales deal or business opportunity.

It is used to track the sales process and possible revenue.

 ##Information Stored in Opportunity

* Deal Amount
* Closing Date
* Sales Stage
* Probability of Success



A company planning to buy software worth ₹5 lakhs.

Resl time example:

If Amazon is discussing purchasing a software product, the sales deal is stored as an Opportunity.



Opportunities help companies track:

* Sales progress
* Revenue
* Business growth

Flow how it works:

Lead → Account + Contact + Opportunity

### Example

1. Customer shows interest → Lead
2. Customer verified → Converted into:

   * Account = Company
   * Contact = Person
   * Opportunity = Sales Deal
